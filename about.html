Multidisciplinary designer from Mumbai, based in New York.

Keyaa Kapadia is a multidisciplinary designer from Mumbai, India, currently based in New York City. She is a senior at Parsons School of Design, majoring in Communication Design, with a practice shaped by curiosity, range, and a love of moving between formats.

She considers herself a generalist, deliberately. Her work stretches across UI/UX, branding, web design, creative coding, presentation design, and visual storytelling, with a special interest in projects that shift between screens, printed matter, and environments.

She's especially drawn to the kind of creative work that asks for versatility: crafting pitch decks and posters, building identity systems, developing early-stage concepts, and imagining immersive experiences for branded, cultural, or spatial contexts. Whether the outcome is polished or still taking shape, she likes finding the connective tissue that makes an idea feel clear, human, and alive.

Her interests also extend into interactive design, emerging digital formats, and the overlap between interface and atmosphere, where a poster can start to feel like a system and a system can start to feel like a place. When she's not designing, you can find her attending concerts, sketching, baking, or starting projects she'll definitely finish later.
