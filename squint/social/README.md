# Squint — social campaign

Keyaa Kapadia

Campaign line: **Less picture. Same feeling.**

A campaign built from the existing Squint artwork and mark. The sequence alternates bold short messages, full-bleed visual studies and an application photograph. Black and paper provide breathing room between pink, teal, acid green and coral.

## Files

- `posts/`: nine individual square PNGs, 1080 × 1080.
- `stories/`: three individual vertical PNGs, 1080 × 1920.
- `motion/`: two silent 8-second colour loops, 24 fps H.264 MP4: a square orb post and a vertical shape story.
- `previews/feed-grid.png`: full-resolution 3 × 3 layout, 3264 × 3264, with slim black gutters.
- `previews/feed-preview.png`: smaller 1500 × 1500 layout preview.
- `previews/social-phone-mockup.png`: photographic campaign presentation on two phones. This is a concept mockup, not a live account screenshot.
- `source/`: self-contained SVG layouts for the posts and stories, with the source artwork embedded. Fonts use Helvetica Neue with Arial fallback. The original project mark is preserved.

The files are independent assets for your case study or social campaign. No website files were changed and nothing was posted to a social account.

## Visual order and suggested captions

Numbers refer to left-to-right, top-to-bottom positions in the feed preview.

1. Launch: “Less picture. Same feeling. Meet Squint.”
2. Orb: “A handful of colours. A whole different picture.”
3. Process: “Look. Count. Squash. See what stays.”
4. Resolution: “How little does a picture need?”
5. Manifesto: “A shape is enough.”
6. Symbols: “Same image. Another language.”
7. Reduction: “Keep less. See more.”
8. In the world: “From pixels to the street.”
9. Closing: “The details change. The feeling stays.”

Story sequence: introduce the campaign; show the type-led pink study; demonstrate resolution becoming coarse blocks. The colour-changing versions can replace the corresponding stills.

## Production and ImageGen prompt

Posts, stories and the grid were composed as native SVG layouts using the existing project artwork and then rendered to PNG. MP4 colour animation was encoded locally. The phone photograph used built-in ImageGen, with the exact feed preview and launch story as reference images.

Final phone-mockup prompt:

One polished photorealistic landscape 3:2 studio photograph presenting a social media campaign on two contemporary thin black smartphones, on a pure black background. Input 1 is the exact nine-post campaign grid for the left phone; preserve the imagery, colors, headlines and three-column tile arrangement. Input 2 is the exact vertical story for the right phone. Left phone slightly taller and further back, right phone in front at a restrained eight-degree angle. Both stand supported by a matte black tabletop, with believable contact shadow and subtle edge light. Screens are large and clear; phones fill 90% of image height. The left screen has a minimal dark profile header reading only “Squint” and the supplied grid, without follower counts, fake engagement or invented handles. Right screen shows the supplied story verbatim. Realistic proportions and restrained reflection; no floating phones, hands, props, presentation labels, watermarks, outside captions or website address. Standalone photographic mockup only.
