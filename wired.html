WIRED

Brand IdentityLogo DesignCreative CodingPrint DesignMerchandise

A WIRED logo that is never the same twice: it moves every minute and changes colour with the day.

Why

WIRED describes itself as always evolving, so a fixed logo felt like the wrong answer.

What I did

I built a generator where each letter is a few points on a line. The points drift with the clock, and the colours come from the day.

What it taught me

A logo can be a rule instead of a shape. Once the rule is clear, every cover, screen and tote is a new frame of the same mark.

Self Initiated · Fall 2026

[p5.js, JavaScript, Figma, Adobe Illustrator, Adobe Photoshop]

Twelve frames from one afternoon

Each frame is the same mark at a different minute, 12:00 to 23:55, with its own palette.
