## Generation notes / final prompt set

Built-in ImageGen was used for the six photographic mockups, with the supplied references for composition and existing Squint poster files as artwork inputs. Video colour animation and encoding were performed locally.

1. Zine black: layered physical art zines on pure black, three open publications and one closed cover; saturated green botanical contours, violet orb and orange tubes; authentic matte paper, curvature and contact shadows; no text or labels.
2. Zine teal: open A5 saddle-stitched booklet over a closed booklet on textured deep teal; full-bleed green contours and violet orb; directional studio lighting, realistic paper and gutter; no text.
3. Night posters: three portrait prints on an almost-black urban storefront, small lamps, restrained pavement reflections; green botanical, violet orb, orange tubes; tight photographic crop; no text.
4. Wheatpaste: three wrinkled pasted prints on weathered brick in afternoon sunlight; green contours, violet orb, cobalt/yellow contours; close oblique photographic view; no text or signage.
5. Zine spreads: four distinct open art-zine spreads in a two-by-two arrangement on pure black; full-bleed and asymmetrical cream-page crops; real paper and binding; no type or labels.
6. Billboard: close photograph of a brushed-aluminum outdoor poster display beside a hedge and modern glass facade; violet orb artwork, warm natural light, slight three-quarter view; no text.
